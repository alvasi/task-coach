# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Quizzes on your recent Claude Code work, as chains: your ticket, the general practice it shows, then that practice in an unrelated scenario.
- Answers about your work must quote your session; questions whose quote isn't really there are thrown away.
- A fact-checked playbook of 92 practices across ten SDLC skills, each with its public source.
- Three-question sittings, confidence ratings, a review queue spaced 1, 3 and 7 days apart, and focused practice per skill.
- Menu bar popover and a full window, with light and dark themes.
